# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS (no framework). Marketing-only single landing page; simplest Coolify static deploy, full control over typography/motion, no build toolchain required.

## Users

Primary: people researching the firm online — prospective clients and partners who need to decide whether Worldwide Invest looks credible and worth contacting.

Open (not locked): whether the eventual book skews high-net-worth individuals vs institutions; the public site speaks to both at a high level.

## Product Purpose

Public marketing presence for Worldwide Invest at worldwide-invest.org. Communicate that the firm is a serious global multi-asset investment company (equities, crypto, energy/oil and related markets), and move institutional / corporate visitors toward partnership contact.

Success: a company that lands on the site (or checks the domain after email from @worldwide-invest.org) respects the firm enough to want a partnership conversation.

## Positioning

Global multi-asset investment house — presence across traditional markets and newer asset classes — not a consumer trading app and not a single-asset crypto brand.

## Operating Context

Server-hosted static site (Coolify). English marketing page first. No client login, portfolio, or trading in v1.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- In scope: single persuasive landing page (brand, markets, about/trust, one contact CTA).
- Out of scope for v1: auth, dashboards, lead-capture backend, CMS, multi-language.
- Contact CTA uses a placeholder channel until real email/phone is supplied.
- Must not invent regulated claims, licenses, AUM figures, performance numbers, client names, awards, or fake testimonials.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Worldwide Invest
- Domain: worldwide-invest.org
- Binding tone (from operator): dark, institutional finance — deep navy/charcoal, sharp type, restrained metal accent. Not light fintech brochure; not purple/glow startup look.
- Logo: not supplied yet.

## Evidence on Hand

None confirmed. No logo file, legal entity name beyond the brand, address, phone, email, case studies, press, or performance data on hand. Future work must use clearly marked placeholders and must not fabricate proof.

## Product Principles

1. Credibility over hype — institutional calm, not retail trading noise.
2. Clarity of markets — visitor can name the asset classes in one glance.
3. One action — contact is the only conversion.
4. Honesty of proof — never invent numbers, clients, or credentials.
5. Simple to host — static deliverable that Coolify can serve without ceremony.
