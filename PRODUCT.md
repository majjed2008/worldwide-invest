# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS (no framework). Marketing presence site; Coolify static deploy.

## Users

Primary: anyone who opens worldwide-invest.org after seeing the brand or an email from the domain — evaluating whether the firm looks serious and premium.

## Product Purpose

Public institutional presence for Worldwide Invest. Present the firm as a global multi-asset investment house (equities, digital assets, energy/commodities). Success: the site feels like a large professional investment company — calm, dense, expensive — with no partnership or sales pitch.

## Positioning

Global multi-asset investment firm. Brand and market presence only — not a retail trading app, not a partnership funnel.

## Operating Context

Coolify static site. English. No login, CMS, or lead forms in v1.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- In scope: single premium landing (brand, strategies/markets, insights tone, about, quiet footer contact).
- Out of scope: partnership CTAs, partner process, partners@ framing, auth, dashboards.
- Contact: quiet footer only (`info@worldwide-invest.org`).
- Must not invent AUM, licenses, performance, clients, awards, or testimonials.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Worldwide Invest
- Domain: worldwide-invest.org
- Visual: dark institutional editorial (premium investment house). No purple/glow fintech. No partnership language on any surface.

## Evidence on Hand

None confirmed beyond brand name and domain. No fabricated proof.

## Product Principles

1. Presence over pitch — the site exists to be respected, not to sell a partnership.
2. Institutional calm and density.
3. Honest silence on numbers we do not have.
4. One quiet contact path in the footer.
5. Simple static hosting.
